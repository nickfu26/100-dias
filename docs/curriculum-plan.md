# 100 Días: curriculum plan, days 11–104 + bonus

Status: **approved 2026-10-07.** Level 2 is written in batches of 5 days. Days 1–10 and checkpoint 1 stay as they are.

Decisions: two lessons per trip day; light days on 24–25 Dec are fine; nationality stays generic (the learner lives in the US, so the tax-free lesson stays). Batch 2 narrowings approved (only some of 21–29 and two months get cards). Age (*¿Cuántos años tienes? / Tengo … años*) gets a card when *años* arrives on day 34. Telling time teaches spoken time and the 24-hour clock used on timetables.

**Trip:** Madrid Wed 13 – Sun 17 Jan 2027, then fly Barajas → Marrakesh on Sun 17 (Marrakesh 17–21 Jan).
**Calendar:** Día 1 = Thu 1 Oct 2026, so Día 100 = Fri 8 Jan 2027 and "Antes del viaje" runs 9–12 Jan.

How to read the tables:
- **Key phrases** are the *new* items for that day. Each one becomes an FSRS card, and each is introduced exactly once in the course (that's a schema rule). Nothing here repeats a word from days 1–10. Those words (*caña* aside: *café, cerveza, vino, zumo, agua, jamón, queso, móvil, hotel, plaza, calle, vale, ¿Cuánto es?, 0–20…*) come back as reviews.
- Spain usage throughout: *vosotros*, *coger*, *móvil*, *billete*, *zumo*, *vale*, *me pones*, *¿me cobras?*. Spain also uses the pretérito perfecto for things that happened today (*hoy he comido…*).
- The last day of each level is a review lesson, and the level's checkpoint test follows it. That matches the existing day 10 + `checkpoint-1.json` pattern.

## Bonus module (outline only): *Marrakech: supervivencia*

Opens 9 Jan. It is optional and kept outside the 100-day sequence. The Darija items will have audio from `ar-MA` edge-tts voices (Jamal / Mouna) and the French items from `fr-FR` voices (Denise / Henri).

**Darija (~20):** salam (alaykum) · labas? · labas, l-hamdu lillah · shukran (bzzaf) · la, shukran (no thanks, the souk essential) · iyyeh / wakha (yes / OK) · afak (please) · smeh liya (excuse me / sorry) · bslama (bye) · ma fhemtsh (I don't understand) · bshhal? (how much?) · ghali bzzaf (too expensive) · nqes shwiya (a bit less) · fin kayn…? (where is…?) · bghit… (I want…) · atay (mint tea) · bla sokkar (no sugar) · lma (water) · safi (enough / that's it) · mezyan (good) · wash kat-hder s-sbanyoliya / l-ingliziya? (do you speak Spanish / English?)

**French (~8):** bonjour · merci (beaucoup) · s'il vous plaît · excusez-moi · combien ça coûte ? · l'addition, s'il vous plaît · je ne comprends pas · vous parlez anglais / espagnol ?
